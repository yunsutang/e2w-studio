About the studio

Where growth meets
intelligence

e2w studio was born at a time when distribution became the moat for early-stage companies. We build e2w studio to help ambitious startups find the right channels, turn signal into growth, and develop the kind of growth instincts that compound over time.

Founded by Yunsu Tang, a former publicist and two-time TEDx speaker, e2w draws on a career spanning early-stage ventures, global communications firms FGS Global and Edelman, and years spent navigating between Hong Kong, Shanghai, and London.

We drive growth through founder-led content, demand generation, community, and IRL campaigns. We combine cross-cultural fluency with modern distribution to help companies turn attention into qualified pipeline.
